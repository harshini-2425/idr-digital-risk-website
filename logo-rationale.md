The IDR logo uses a cube-inspired geometric mark to represent structure, interconnected systems, and resilience under pressure, which aligns with digital and AI risk practice. The orange, black, and white palette was chosen for strong contrast and a modern technical character, while keeping the identity clean and minimal for education and industry contexts. A compact icon-only variant supports small sizes such as favicon and mobile headers, and the full lockup variant adds the Institute name for formal communications. The monospaced wordmark styling reinforces precision and discipline, reflecting IDR's industry-led and framework-driven approach.
